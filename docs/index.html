A proudly Bittensor-gnostic framework for building production-ready subnets

Subnets should be created with agents, not hand-built from scratch.

Const's Chi helps shape the Bittensor-native design. Nexus gives agents and developers the production framework to build, test, operate, and evolve it. The Catnet comparison shows the difference on one concrete subnet.

Solve common subnet plumbing once, then let every Nexus-based subnet inherit it.

Every capability built into Nexus becomes available to every Nexus-based subnet. Bittensor already has great subnets with impressive tooling and engineering; Nexus can make that level of operational quality much more common, narrowing the gap between the best-run subnets and the average subnet.
